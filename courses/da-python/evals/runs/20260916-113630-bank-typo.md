Notes:
The False Positives decreased from {fp} to {fp2} because raising the threshold (T=0.65) demands higher confidence before predicting a positive outcome (subscription). When the call budget is limited, this is critical because a False Positive represents a wasted call on a non-subscriber, meaning a higher threshold increases precision (fewer wasted calls) at the cost of lower recall (more genuine subscribers slipping through as False Negatives).
